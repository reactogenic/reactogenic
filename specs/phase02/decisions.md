# Phase 2 decisions

Taken on 2026-10-04 from the research ([research.md](research.md)), while the
owner was away: each is the research's recommended choice — except 16 to
20, which the specs, their review and the integration took against it or
without it — with what would reverse it. **For review** marks the ones that
touch something CLAUDE.md fixes or that are a matter of taste. What was
*not* decided then is at the end: *For the owner* — ruled since, but for A
and K.

What decision 3 costs, so that it is known when it is looked at again: a
project needs `react` and `react-dom` installed to build, also with no
dynamic segment in it; and the builder knows React's static renderer in two
places — a file outside `react-dom`'s `exports`, and one call in it that the
builder routes through itself to count components (builder.md, *The
record*). Only 19.3.0 is tested; a React that moves either stops the build
with `render-bundle`. The other way is a serializer of our own with
`react-dom/server` as the test oracle (the differential test exists):
shell components would be plain functions, with no hook at all.
